Thanksgiving Day Holiday Reminder

Date: November 18, 2026
To: Hourly Associates - Teamsters
From: Distribution Management
Subject: Thanksgiving Day Holiday - Reminder

Just a reminder with the holiday coming up, Thanksgiving Holiday Thursday, November 26th the following language from the CBA states:

“In order to be entitled to holiday pay, an employee must work their scheduled workday immediately preceding, the day of the holiday, if scheduled; and immediately following the holiday, except for excusable absences such as illness, or injury or leave of absence.”

In the event an associate calls in absent on any of the days indicated above, holiday pay will be suppressed until the associate provides a doctor's note to the supervisor. Upon receipt of the Doctor’s note, the Time & Attendance Administrator will process any holiday benefit pay that may have been earned.

Please refer any questions to your location HR Manager.

Thank you and have a happy and safe Thanksgiving Holiday!
