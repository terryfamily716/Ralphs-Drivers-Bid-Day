Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy applies to Ralph's Drivers Bid Day Live, located at terryfamily716.github.io/Ralphs-Drivers-Bid-Day, operated by Larry Terry.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. When you create a driver profile, we store your first name, last name, company selection, and role. DVIR inspection data, including defect photos and odometer readings, may also be stored.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide driver inspection tools, bid day scheduling, driver communication, and fleet management features. We do not sell your information.

Google Sign-In

Sign-in is handled by Google. Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy governs how Google processes your account data.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count page visits. GoatCounter does not use cookies or collect personal data.

Data Retention

DVIR records are retained for a minimum of 90 days per FMCSA regulations. Profile data is retained while your account is active.
